Next Best Action for Commercial & Institutional Banking

Be the bank that calls before the client asks.

A working system that reads a client's own transaction behavior alongside what's happening in their market — and hands the Relationship Manager one ranked, evidence-backed reason to pick up the phone, before a competitor does.

The problem

Every bank has the data. Almost none of it gets acted on in time.

A Relationship Manager covers dozens, sometimes hundreds, of commercial and institutional clients. The signals that matter — a client just received an unusually large payment, a public tender just opened up their sector, a rate move just made their treasury balance worth a conversation — already exist, scattered across transaction systems and public information nobody has time to watch client-by-client.

"By the time we noticed, the client had already gone to someone else."

Outreach today is largely calendar-driven, not event-driven. The cost isn't visible on any single day — it shows up as the renewal that should have been a cross-sell, the treasury surplus sitting idle for months, the client who felt like a number instead of a relationship.

What comes out

One ranked recommendation. Plain language. Full evidence attached.

Not a dashboard full of numbers — a single card per opportunity, the way an RM would actually want to receive it. Every claim on the card traces back to a specific, checkable fact. Nothing is invented, and the system never contacts the client itself — it hands the decision to the person who should be making it.

Why the bank should care

The same signal that grows revenue is the one that protects it.

Profit

Earlier, better-targeted conversations

RM time goes to the clients most likely to need a product right now — financing, hedging, treasury — instead of being spread evenly across a book where most clients need nothing this week.

Trust

The bank notices first

A client who's called about something relevant before they raised it themselves experiences the relationship differently than one who's called on a quarterly script. That's retention, not just conversion.

Protection

The same system flags risk, not just opportunity

Sanctions exposure, credit stress, counterparty concentration surface through the identical mechanism — early awareness protects the bank's book, not only grows it.

Discipline

Built to be governed, not retrofitted

Every recommendation is traceable to its evidence. A human reviews before anything reaches a client. The system that measures results is kept separate from the system that produces them, from day one — not bolted on after an audit finds a problem.
